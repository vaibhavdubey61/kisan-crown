Frequently Asked Questions

How easy is it to change screens between wheat and corn?

Screen changes take less than 3 minutes. Simply loosen the two side butterfly clamps, slide out the current screen deck, and slide in the new crop screen.

Can it remove mud balls and small stones?

Yes! The multi-deck reciprocating screen separates oversized stones on the top deck and drops small sand/dust through the bottom fine screen.
