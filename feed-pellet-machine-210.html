BUYER & DEALER FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

What ingredients can be pelleted in this machine?

Crushed maize, wheat bran, soybean meal, mustard de-oiled cake, dried fish meal, bone meal, rice polish, molasses, and mineral mixtures.

How do pellets improve animal digestion?

Pelletizing uses natural frictional heat (approx. 70°C) which gelatinizes raw starches, kills harmful salmonella bacteria, and prevents animals from selectively picking ingredients, ensuring 100% balanced nutrition.

What is the lifespan of the pellet die and rollers?

Under normal operation, our hardened alloy steel dies last for 300 to 500 tonnes of feed before needing reversal or replacement. Replacement dies are always in stock in Raipur.
