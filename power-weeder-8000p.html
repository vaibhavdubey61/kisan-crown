BUYER & DEALER FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the differential lock work?

Hand levers on the handlebar disengage the drive to either the left or right wheel, allowing the machine to rotate smoothly on its axis in tight orchard and crop rows.

Can this machine be used in wet mud for paddy puddling?

Yes! By replacing the rubber tires with our optional steel cage puddling wheels, it works as an exceptional mini-puddler in flooded paddy fields.
